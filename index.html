01Introduction

Ease of Mind Apps ("we", "us", or "our") provides SmartSTB, a media player application that allows users to access and play IPTV streams using playlists or credentials supplied by the users themselves.

SmartSTB does not provide, host, sell, or distribute television channels, playlists, subscriptions, or media content.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed and processed when using SmartSTB.

02Information Processed by the App

Depending on the features used, SmartSTB may process:

  • IPTV playlist URLs supplied by users.
  • IPTV service credentials entered by users.
  • Media stream addresses and metadata received from user-selected IPTV services.
  • Technical information required for playback, network connectivity, and application functionality.

The storage location, retention period, and any transmission of this information to developer-controlled services are subject to technical verification.

03How Information Is Used

Information entered into SmartSTB is used to enable users to connect to their selected IPTV services, retrieve available playlists or media information, and play compatible content.

SmartSTB may communicate with IPTV servers selected by users to perform these functions.

04Third-Party Services

SmartSTB connects to IPTV services configured by users. Those providers may independently process information, including network addresses, connection details, and account credentials, according to their own privacy policies.

The use of additional third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or infrastructure services has not yet been verified and must be disclosed here if present.

05Data Security

We recognize the importance of protecting user information. The application's credential storage, transmission security, and other technical safeguards must be verified before final publication of this policy.

Users should only enter credentials for services they trust and are authorized to access.

06Data Retention and Deletion

The app's precise storage and deletion behavior is currently being verified.

Users may contact appsforcomfort@gmail.com with privacy or deletion inquiries. The available deletion procedures and applicable retention periods will be described here once confirmed.

07Children's Privacy

SmartSTB is a general-purpose media player. The content available through the application depends on the IPTV services and playlists selected by users.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's access to third-party media services.

08Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the application, its technical functionality, or applicable legal requirements.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
